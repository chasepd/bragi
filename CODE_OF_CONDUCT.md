# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Bragi contributors and maintainers commit to a welcoming, harassment-free
community. Be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive feedback, and
focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone represents the Bragi project.

## Enforcement

Report platform abuse through GitHub's private reporting tools. Submit a
sensitive project-moderation report through the repository's private reporting
form and begin the title with `Code of Conduct`:

https://github.com/chasepd/bragi/security/advisories/new

This private form is also used for security reports, but conduct reports do not
need to describe a vulnerability. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participants whose conduct is inconsistent with this
standard. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical, and
retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
